# Vietnamese reader evidence extension

The prespecified milestone adds 24 public travel-decision submissions to the previous 36 qualifying Vietnamese-language cases. A qualifying case has a reader or citizen question attributed by its publisher and an applicability-matched Vietnamese evidence source issued independently of the submission and its published reply. A separately published regulatory instrument can qualify even when the question host also mirrors the instrument. Attribution is based on public publisher metadata and wording. It does not authenticate submitter identity. Excerpts preserve wording apart from Unicode and whitespace normalization. For multipart submissions, an explicitly scoped decision must stand on its own or be marked as a scoped evidence check. Sources and extracted cards are linked in the audit files, with identifiers and page ranges. The cohort is purposive and concentrated in archived air, rail and border procedure questions. The archived scope is tied to the original article or named statutory change. It is not a current travel advisory. The same explicit applicability date and any necessary context clipped from the reader submission are supplied to retrieval and every reranker. The original reader excerpt and the machine input are each stored.

The bounded corpus appends independently issued evidence to the 413 previously collected Vietnamese passages. Mechanically retrieve an unfiltered BM25 top five from this library. Judge whether each retrieved passage independently resolves the scoped decision under the relevant conditions and cutoff date. Grade 2 is sufficient. Grade 1 offers applicable but incomplete evidence. Grade 0 is inapplicable or uninformative. Ambiguous slots remain unresolved and cannot establish an availability failure. If a verified sufficient passage exists in the library and none of the five native candidates is sufficient, replace only the fifth with that passage. Denominators and panel membership are fixed before checkpoint scoring. Use identical frozen pools for the seven checkpoints. Record top-one sufficient-evidence count, selection failures on available-evidence pools, and conditional controlled replacement success. Publish exact counts and score-file lineage. The extra cohort is exploratory and has a single desk audit. Its evidence references are distinct from the reader answers, with visibly marked joins when a decision requires provisions spanning sections or issuances.

The total count includes previous cohorts, but the new cohort has its own corpus snapshot and panel. Do not pool effect sizes across different snapshots or generalize to open-web retrieval or generation. No parameter fitting or checkpoint selection uses these case outcomes.
