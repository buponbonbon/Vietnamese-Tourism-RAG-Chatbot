# Further public-reader cohort

The target is 60–100 unique attributable real questions with an independently
published Vietnamese supporting passage. This is a target for the cumulative
cohort. The current expansion uses public reader articles, comment records and
citizen submissions. Each item retains a question locator, source digest and a
documentary evidence locator. Exact duplicate questions, paraphrased variants,
editorial FAQs with unverified origin, and itinerary requests without a
resolvable factual subdecision are ineligible.

Where a long question contains several requests, decide on one factual
subdecision before scoring and record that scope. Retain the reader's words,
with a separate original headline if the question needs the publication
context. General policies can be evaluated under an expressly named archived
snapshot. Event-specific decisions require an applicable period. Passage
sufficiency for a conditional travel option covers the named operator and
conditions. Venue-level claims need an authoritative operator or government
description. Reused reader questions and independent authors from the same
article are accounted for separately. Corpus review is a project documentary
audit, not a new multiple-annotator panel.

Use the earlier closed Vietnamese library plus licensed local research excerpts
from new independent sources. The five BM25 candidates are frozen before scoring.
Judgments distinguish independently sufficient, insufficient, and unresolved
candidates. If all five are confirmed insufficient, replace the fifth with a
verified passage and keep the other four. Report bounded top-one sufficiency if
a relevant rating is unresolved. Report the old and new cohorts separately.

The new stage 5 schedule transfer is independently evaluated. Its generated
questions do not enter the genuine-reader cohort.
