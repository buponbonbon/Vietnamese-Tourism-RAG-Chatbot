# Reader-question evidence audit protocol

Recorded before scoring on 21 November 2026. The unit is a Vietnamese reader's question that was published by VnExpress together with a visible first-person submission or a reader byline. These are edited, public submissions. There is no claim about the reader's identity or the text sent before editing.

## Population and inclusion

- The sampling frame is the first six archived pages of the public VnExpress travel Q&A category fetched in this study, in publication order. One eligible decision per article. Log each story's disposition, including stories without reader provenance, editorial explainers and retrieval failures.
- Include a factual travel decision for which an official agency, operator or policy document outside the question publication directly supports one answer or the material conditions for answering it. Prioritize entry, travel documents, transport access, departures and passenger entitlements. Screen out personal recommendations, predictions of visa success, extraordinary time-sensitive circumstances without a contemporaneous reference, and itinerary-specific cases for which a deciding detail is absent. Do not use answers, headlines or comments from the query publication as evidence.
- Score a contiguous span of the published question's own prose, preserving details necessary for the selected decision, with whitespace normalized. Separate independent secondary questions only where a contiguous question span retains necessary context. Record exact content, story URI, span locator, publication date, and extracted source evidence; do not retain author names in model inputs. Avoid selecting questions or changing any inclusion, context span, evidence or distractor after viewing model outputs.
- For explicit regime/date constraints retain the period and use documentation that covers it. Date-neutral policy questions are examined on the archived authority snapshot available to this experiment. Do not retrospectively treat the snapshot as advice valid on the original submission date.
- Use source language as published; document cross-language matching. Use source-page titles or headings as context where applicable. The human-submission language is Vietnamese. This diagnostic uses the official jurisdictions the readers asked about, which may be outside Vietnam.
- For each case, designate a sufficient source passage and one near distractor that addresses a similar decision but lacks a required condition or applies to another case. Add three other nonsufficient passages with themes or terms plausibly matching the question. Candidates may be excerpts assembled from adjacent source blocks plus heading. All five must be in the same evidence language per question. Verify that each candidate is distinct and that no distractor independently supports the scoped decision. Record explicit reasons. If a candidate pool cannot be verified, omit the case and log the reason.
- The evidence judgments are a single source-based audit by the study assistant against independent published rules. They do not imply independent annotations by two raters. Evidence must specify its applicable carrier, jurisdiction or regime. A policy illustration for a named provider is accepted only when the query asks whether an option exists, where to find a source, or when the relevant policy is common and the document explicitly supports it.

## Comparison

Evaluate frozen BGE pretrained and matched M0/M1 seed 42, 123 and 999 in CPU FP32 with no model training, plus symmetric Unicode BM25. Use the same five excerpts per query, same scoring inputs and strict first rank. The two-excerpt endpoint compares the sufficient excerpt with the prespecified near distractor. The full-pool endpoint requires score greater than all four distractors. A strict tie is failure in both endpoints. Count pair passes lost after adding candidates; list the winning wrong passage and missed constraint. Report all cases, seed-specific counts, paired comparative differences and scope. This is a fixed-availability diagnostic; it does not estimate corpus retrieval recall or user preferences. There is no confirmatory test or population error claim from this selected, single-outlet cohort. Archive raw logits, protocol and reviewed provenance.
