# Vietnamese-operator extension

Recorded 21 November 2026 before model scoring. The goal is to verify the same-candidate competition finding on new Vietnamese reader questions where all passages are also Vietnamese. This is a source-grounded stress test.

## Screening and candidate construction

- Use first-person reader submissions or explicit operational questions in public reader comments. Preserve contiguous wording from the published article body or comment, with whitespace normalization and no author identifiers in model inputs. Adjacent first-person paragraphs may be joined. A question from the article headline alone is ineligible. For replies or comments that depend on context, prepend the exact published headline as its own line and retain the original question wording. Archive article URL and the text locator or comment ID. Log editorial or hypothetical queries, comments expressing opinions and question duplicates as exclusions.
- Require an independently published Vietnamese source by the operator, airport or competent public authority. It must resolve the scoped operational decision, not just mention the service. A question about a named operator must match that operator. A question asking for any option may be supported by one verified operator. Scope current or historical policies consistently and exclude unresolved station, carrier, traveler, eligibility or date requirements. A dated authority announcement is admissible for the regime it explicitly covers. Original story answers and comments are never candidate evidence.
- Keep the entire case pool in Vietnamese. Preserve sourced passage wording, recording the exact source and locator. Each case has one independently sufficient passage, one prespecified nearby insufficient passage that concerns the same service, decision or a plausible alternative, and three additional insufficient passages. Exclude a case if the scope or insufficiency of any candidate is unresolved. Prioritize different rail, bus, aviation and cable/ferry operator groups and log what source material was screened. Distinct decisions may come from a common discussion. Report that source dependence.
- Add only cases absent from the nine-case first pilot. Freeze all cases, source provenance and labels before viewing any reranker output to avoid selecting by outcome. Use one set of judgments from an AI research assistant anchored to source passages. No independent new human annotation is represented.

## Evaluation

- Each question sees exactly two or five of the same fixed candidates. A successful pair comparison requires the sufficient passage score to strictly exceed the prespecified near distractor. For five candidates, it must strictly exceed all four distractors. Report the number of pair successes lost at five, each scorer's outcomes and the M1-minus-M0 differences within seeds.
- Evaluate the same BGE pretrained checkpoint and M0/M1 seeds 42, 123, 999, with archived input hash verification and CPU FP32 inference. Add Unicode BM25 with IDF over the fixed candidate corpus. Use the same pointwise logits in both endpoints. Ties fail. Archive inputs, all logits, traceable labels and report. Results apply only to the tested pools. Compare the new cohort separately from the earlier nine cases. No causal inference about deployed utility or prevalence from this purposive sample.
